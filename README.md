# Subtext: Beyond the Algorithm 🎬

## 1. What the Product Solves
Traditional streaming platforms rely on shallow genre filters (e.g., "Action," "Drama") that often fail to capture the soul of a film. Subtext fills the gap where:

* **Users reject basic genre filtering:** They seek recommendations based on specific **themes, politics, tone, and style**.
* **Mainstream fatigue:** There is a need to discover films that exist **beyond the mainstream catalogs** and "Top 10" trending lists.
* **Contextual Depth:** Users don't just want a list; they want to understand *why* a film fits their specific intellectual or emotional mood.

---

## 2. User Personas
The product is designed for viewers who treat cinema as an art form and a social tool:

* **The Cinephile:** A film lover who has exhausted the basics and is looking for the next cult classic or obscure masterpiece.
* **The Politicized Viewer:** A user seeking content with social commentary, historical accuracy, or a specific political lens.
* **Indie / Festival Audience:** Viewers who follow circuits like Cannes, Berlin, or Sundance and prefer independent productions over blockbusters.
* **The Curation-Seeker:** Someone tired of "infinite scrolling" who wants a personalized recommendation with a human-like justification.

---

## 3. The v1 Scope (MVP)
The first version of CineCurator focuses on the core logic of discovery rather than interface complexity:

* **Natural Language Query:** Accepts queries in plain, conversational language.
* **Curated Results:** Returns **3 to 5** highly relevant films.
* **Reasoning Engine:** Explains **why** each film matches the query (the "connective tissue").
* **Availability:** Mentions streaming or rental availability **only if** the data is accessible.

---
